# Product brief

## User and job

Who has the problem? What are they trying to finish, and how often?

## First useful result

What can the user accomplish in the first session? What existing Apple feature or competing app is the baseline?

## Return loop

What changes or accumulates that makes the app useful again? What evidence will show a real return habit?

## Payment rationale

What valuable capability is paid? Why is a one-time purchase, subscription, or free model appropriate? For a subscription, describe the continuing value.

## Non-goals and constraints

Record the features, platforms, data, and costs deliberately excluded from the first release.
