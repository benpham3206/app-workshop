## Patterns and implications

- **FACT — there is no public basis here to say “more than 70% of the top 20 ship surface X.”** Published chart rankings do not include an app-by-app capability audit, and the requested cross-platform set is not a single ranked population. The iPhone-only chart is not evidence about Mac, Watch, or widgets.
- **INFERENCE — distribution and grossing diverge.** The public 2025 download leaders include free AI assistants, browser, PDF, and authenticator utilities. Current US iPhone grossing charts include subscription-led AI, cloud storage, and office products. This suggests utility distribution can be broad while revenue concentrates in services; it does **not** show that a particular Apple surface causes revenue.
- **INFERENCE — widgets and OS entry points fit frequent, glanceable jobs** such as tasks, schedules, capture, timers, and flight status. Structured, Things, Tiimo, Raycast, and Flighty illustrate this pattern. Live Activities fit time-bounded states such as a focus session or flight; that is a product-design inference, not evidence that Live Activities improve grossing.
- **FACT — observed pricing patterns are mixed:** the cohort includes free tiers with subscriptions, per-platform one-time purchases (Things), a lifetime option (Structured), and a free core app with paid sync/publishing services (Obsidian). Example trial lengths found: 7 days for Tiimo’s annual plan and CleanMyMac’s trial; 14 days for Fantastical. These examples do not establish an industry-wide median.
- **FACT — known annual/monthly examples:** TickTick’s US listing shows $4.99 monthly and $49.99 yearly. Todoist shows $7 monthly or $60 yearly; there is no conflict **[FC]**.
- **UNKNOWN — trial-to-paid conversion, annual-vs-monthly subscriber mix, lifetime-purchase share, and platform-level revenue split** for the requested apps. Public store listings and estimates checked here do not establish these figures.

**INFERENCE — For App Workshop:** The market evidence supports treating customer and creator operations as essential factory concerns, but it does not justify adding every surface to every generated app. Keep the selected app’s user job as the gate for widgets, Live Activities, Watch, App Intents, collaboration, and AI. Use the requested RevenueCat default as an owner decision; the cited ranking data does not establish that RevenueCat or any particular purchase stack increases revenue.

## Evidence boundary

**FACT — checked 2026-10-19:** Download figures above are third-party estimates; revenue figures are either a cited ranking statement or third-party figures whose periods are not disclosed. Storefront and platform claims come from Apple’s App Store listings or app/vendor documentation. `UNKNOWN` entries are intentional evidence gaps. Mac implementation technology (native, Catalyst, Electron) is generally not disclosed in the cited app listings; confirm it separately before treating it as a factory-wide pattern.

**Skipped / not checked:** No paid market-intelligence account was used, no 2025 revenue estimate was substituted with a current unperiodized figure, and the full feature matrix was not independently tested on devices. The main risk is comparing estimates with different geographies, periods, and store coverage as if they were directly comparable.
