# Fact-check of lanes 01–05 (gpt-6.1-sol, high effort)

Checked against live primary sources on **2026-10-19**. I read all five `*.out.md` lane outputs. The main corrections are the RevenueCat release number, the claimed Todoist pricing conflict, and several missing date/platform distinctions.

1. **CONFIRMED — Customer Center.** RevenueCat explicitly supports iOS 15+ and Mac Catalyst 15+, and explicitly excludes **native macOS**. Refund requests and subscription-plan changes are marked iOS-only. “Not on macOS” needs the native-versus-Catalyst distinction. [Supported platforms](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/customer-center/customer-center-installation), [features](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/customer-center).

2. **CONFIRMED — Paywalls UI.** The documented Apple platforms are iOS 15+, Mac Catalyst 15+, and native macOS 12+; watchOS, tvOS, and visionOS are excluded. Native macOS support starts with `purchases-ios` **5.39.0**. [Platform matrix](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/paywalls), [macOS requirements](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/paywalls/displaying-paywalls).

3. **PARTLY — SDK release and SPM URL.** The recommended SPM URL is correct. **Correction:** GitHub’s live latest release is **5.96.0**, released October 16, 2026, rather than 5.91.0. [Latest release](https://github.com/RevenueCat/purchases-ios/releases/tag/5.96.0), [SPM installation](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/getting-started/installation/ios).

4. **CONFIRMED — RevenueCat pricing.** Standard Pro pricing advertises free usage up to $2,500 MTR, then **1% of the whole tracked amount**, rather than only the excess. Its billing example charges $26 on $2,600 MTR, before store commission and taxes. Enterprise/custom agreements can differ. [Pricing](https://www.revenuecat.com/pricing), [billing explanation](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/welcome/set-up-revenuecat/account-management).

5. **CONFIRMED — REST API v2.** The base is `https://api.revenuecat.com/v2`; v2 secret keys have selectable permissions, and v1 keys do not work. Under `/projects/{project_id}`, the documented resources include `metrics/overview`, `customers`, `subscriptions`, and `subscriptions/{subscription_id}/transactions`. Charts & Metrics share a **25 requests/minute** default limit. [Authentication and limits](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2), [metrics](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2/charts-and-metrics), [customers](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2/customer), [subscriptions](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2/subscription), [transactions](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/api-v2/subscription-transactions).

6. **CONFIRMED — App Store Connect report keys.** Apple explicitly says Individual keys cannot access Sales and Finance. Those operations require a Team key with appropriate permissions; merely having any Team key does not establish authorization. [Apple’s API-key documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreconnectapi/creating-api-keys-for-app-store-connect-api).

7. **CONFIRMED — Xcode/iOS versions, with a same-day update.** Xcode 27.1 was released October 15 and includes Swift 6.4 and the iOS 27.1 SDK. Apple’s dated entry confirms Xcode 27.2 beta 3 on October 14. iOS 27.0.1 remains the latest stable iOS shown. **However, Xcode 27.2 RC and iOS 27.2 RC are now listed for October 19**, so beta 3 is no longer the latest prerelease. [Release list](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/), [SDK/compiler table](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/system-requirements/), [beta 3 entry](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/?id=10142026b).

8. **CONFIRMED — April 2027 SDK requirement.** Apple says iOS/iPadOS uploads must use the iOS/iPadOS **27 SDK or later**, and target iOS 15 or later, starting April 2027. This does **not** specifically mandate SDK 27.1. [Submission requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/submitting/).

9. **CONFIRMED — iPhone Duo.** Apple gives October 23, 2026 availability, explains that building with the iOS 27.1 SDK extends presentation to the screen edge, and states that Duo screenshots become required beginning April 2027. [Launch/submission announcement](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo), [SDK presentation explanation](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/111461/), [screenshot requirement](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/).

10. **CONFIRMED — Bitrig, as documented by its maker.** Bitrig describes its Mac development app/Agent and iPhone Remote companion, lists Pro at **US$15/month**, and documents App Store publishing and RevenueCat integration. Its April 7 post says Apple found the original iPhone app noncompliant with §2.5.2. Attribute that rejection account to **Bitrig**; the checked evidence is its own report. AI usage and Apple Developer membership are separate cost considerations. [Pricing/product](https://bitrig.com/pricing), [Remote and rejection explanation](https://bitrig.com/blog/meet-bitrig-remote), [publishing](https://bitrig.com/blog/bitrig-publishes-to-the-app-store), [RevenueCat integration](https://bitrig.com/blog/adds-subscription-iap-and-revenuecat).

11. **CONFIRMED — Xcode 26.3 agents.** Apple’s **February 3, 2026** announcement names Anthropic’s Claude Agent and OpenAI’s Codex. On that announcement date, 26.3 was a **release candidate**, with the App Store release still forthcoming. [Apple Newsroom announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/02/xcode-26-point-3-unlocks-the-power-of-agentic-coding/).

12. **CONFIRMED — Alex, but supply the year.** The team announced joining OpenAI’s Codex team on **September 2, 2025** and set the new-download cutoff for **October 1, 2025**. The current homepage says downloads stopped. This was not an October 2026 event. [Dated team announcement](https://www.alexcodes.app/blog/alex-team-joins-openai), [current homepage](https://www.alexcodes.app/).

13. **CONFIRMED — Foundation Models’ WWDC26 expansion.** Apple documents the on-device model, `PrivateCloudComputeLanguageModel`, and a `LanguageModel` protocol supporting third-party/local/server implementations. The framework itself predates WWDC26; these are expansions to it. Availability, entitlements, authentication, and billing depend on the selected model. [WWDC26 session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/241/), [framework reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/FoundationModels).

14. **CONFIRMED — MetricKit documentation.** Apple’s current `MXMetricManager` reference marks it deprecated and directs developers to `MetricManager`. This confirms the **documentation claim**; it does not establish that every SDK header produces a compiler deprecation warning. [MXMetricManager reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metrickit/mxmetricmanager).

15. **CONFIRMED — adoption figure; the precise source is Apple’s environmental report.** **Page 45 of the 2026 Environmental Progress Report** says that **by February 2026**, more than 74% of iPhones introduced in the preceding four years had updated to iOS 26. February is the measurement reference, not necessarily the report’s publication date. The current App Store support page instead reports June 7 measurements: **86%** for that recent-device cohort and **79%** across all iPhones measured. [2026 report, p. 45](https://www.apple.com/environment/pdf/Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2026.pdf), [current App Store adoption page](https://developer.apple.com/support/app-store/).

Replace the staging citation with the production article. [Production pricing page](https://www.todoist.com/pricing), [production pricing-update article](https://www.todoist.com/help/account-and-billing/plans/todoist-pro-pricing-update-in-2025-bxBvHZuJZ).

17. **CONFIRMED — analytics allowances.** TelemetryDeck reduced new accounts to **50,000 events/signals per month** effective July 1, 2026; accounts created before that date retain 100,000 under the stated grandfathering terms. Aptabase advertises **20,000 events/month** free. PostHog advertises **1 million analytics events/month** free; its other products have separate allowances. [TelemetryDeck’s dated change](https://telemetrydeck.com/blog/pricing-update-2026/), [Aptabase pricing](https://aptabase.com/pricing), [PostHog pricing](https://posthog.com/pricing).

**Other material issues found in the lane outputs:**

- **`r1-market.out.md`: Things sync is incorrectly described as “Things Cloud/iCloud sync.”** Cultured Code explicitly says iCloud and other third-party sync services are unsupported. Use **Things Cloud**; device backup through iCloud is a separate matter. [Things’ sync documentation](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2803586/).

- **`r2-surfaces.out.md`: `ShareLink` is suggested in the implementation for incoming quick capture.** `ShareLink` presents an **outbound** sharing interface. Receiving content from another app’s share sheet requires an appropriate receiving extension; `ShareLink` does not supply that path. [Apple’s ShareLink reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/sharelink?changes=latest_major).

- **`r4-ops.out.md`: the MetricKit wording groups daily metrics and diagnostics too broadly.** Daily metric delivery and diagnostic delivery have different timing: Apple says diagnostics arrive immediately on supported systems. It remains fair to distinguish MetricKit from a hosted crash-alerting service, but “daily” should not be applied indiscriminately to diagnostics. [MetricKit timing documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metrickit?changes=_6__7).

- **`r5-apis.out.md`: “App Store Connect began accepting Xcode 27.1 builds on October 15” is too late.** Apple’s October 5 entry already permits App Store uploads built with **Xcode 27.1 RC**. October 15 records acceptance of the final release. [App Store Connect release notes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/).
