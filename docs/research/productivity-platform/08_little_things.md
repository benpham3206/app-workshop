# Apple productivity app “little things” catalog

> Orchestrator notes (2026-10-19): (1) Customer Center also runs on Mac Catalyst 15+, not on native macOS (see [`06_factcheck.md`](06_factcheck.md) #1). (2) Switch 16 below, the Home Screen quick action, was added from Ben's request; it is not lane research and needs a fact-check.

**Decision — INFERENCE:** Make customer care and retention a set of small, opt-in capabilities selected per app. Keep recovery and data protection as core behavior; make marketing, retention messages, analytics, referrals, and alternate branding off by default. Use StoreKit and Apple’s subscription-management flows for Apple purchases, RevenueCat as the project’s chosen purchase layer, and RevenueCat Customer Center for its supported in-app support paths. A retention discount at cancel time is possible, but it is a configured offer with eligibility and signing requirements—not a callback when someone deletes an app.

**Evidence date:** Web sources checked **2026-10-19**, unless a source date is stated. FACT means the linked Apple or vendor source describes the capability or rule; INFERENCE means a design recommendation or acceptance check; UNKNOWN means the source review did not establish the answer. The acceptance checks below are proposed checks, not Apple approval guarantees.

## 1. App deletion and the nearest useful moments

**UNKNOWN — no documented public app-uninstall callback was found.** Apple’s app lifecycle documentation covers foreground, background, termination, and other system events; it does not document an uninstall event. That supports treating app deletion as unsupported by a public hook, but does not prove that no private or internal mechanism exists. [UIKit app lifecycle](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/managing-your-app-s-life-cycle/) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

| Moment | What the app or service can do |
|---|---|
| **Before the customer leaves the app to cancel** | **FACT:** Show a clear manage-subscription route; offer Customer Center feedback or a retention offer, or link to Apple’s subscription manager. Cancellation still needs to remain straightforward. [App Review Guidelines §3.1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19); [Customer Center](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/customer-center) (RevenueCat, checked 2026-10-19). |
| **In Apple’s system cancellation flow** | **FACT:** Apple Retention Messaging can show an approved retention message, offer, or alternate plan when the customer is viewing subscription details and may cancel. This is a server-to-server Apple flow, not an app uninstall callback. [Endpoint setup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/setting-up-retention-messaging-endpoint) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19). |
| **Cancellation from Settings / Subscription settings** | **FACT:** App Store Server Notifications can report subscription renewal-status changes. Treat this as a server event, not an opportunity to interrupt the cancellation flow or infer that the app was deleted. [Handling subscription billing](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/StoreKit/handling-subscriptions-billing) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19). |
| **Billing failure or lapse** | **FACT:** The app can respond to verified subscription state; Apple retries billing and may grant configured billing grace. RevenueCat webhooks can report cancellation and expiration reasons, including billing errors. [Apple billing](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/StoreKit/handling-subscriptions-billing), [RevenueCat webhook event types](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/webhooks/event-types-and-fields) (checked 2026-10-19). |
| **Account deletion** | **FACT:** Account deletion is a separate in-app action for apps that support account creation. It is not app removal, and must not be replaced by a survey or support-contact requirement. [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19). |
| **Later win-back or web contact** | **FACT:** Eligible lapsed subscribers may find Apple win-back offers in the App Store, Apple Account subscription settings, the app, or a direct link. A web account/support flow is separately designed by the developer; it cannot detect an App Store app deletion. [Apple win-back setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-win-back-offers) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19). |

## 2. Apple offers and cancellation-time discounts

### Retention Messaging API

**FACT:** Apple’s changelog dates the API’s initial pre-release to **2025-07-16**. That supports calling it a 2025 pre-release, not claiming a generally shipped 2025 feature; Apple’s current rollout materials describe a 2026 App Store Connect rollout. [Apple Retention Messaging changelog](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/retention-messaging-changelog) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

**FACT:** The API serves messages in Apple’s subscription cancellation flow. It supports text, text with an image, an alternate subscription plan, and a promotional offer. For real-time selection, Apple calls the developer’s endpoint as the customer views subscription details where they may cancel. The chosen message and any image must be approved. [Endpoint setup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/setting-up-retention-messaging-endpoint), [real-time response](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/responding-to-realtime-retention-messaging-requests), [offer response](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/promotionaloffer) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

**FACT:** A developer-operated real-time endpoint requires HTTPS, a valid certificate, TLS 1.2, a successful sandbox performance test, and a response within Apple’s documented 700 ms production timeout. Apple provides a default-message fallback; if no usable fallback exists, a message may not display. [Endpoint setup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/setting-up-retention-messaging-endpoint) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

**FACT:** RevenueCat documents support for Apple Retention Messaging, including operating the real-time connection, configuring and localizing messages, and associating offers or alternate plans. RevenueCat says the feature requires Apple access approval and is in pre-release; its docs list iOS 15.1+, iPadOS 15.1+, visionOS 1+, and macOS 14+ message visibility. **UNKNOWN:** The reviewed sources do not establish watchOS support. RevenueCat’s retention-message charts require App Store Server Notifications for accurate analytics, though displaying messages does not. [RevenueCat Apple Retention Messaging](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/platform-resources/apple-platform-resources/apple-retention-messaging-api) (RevenueCat, checked 2026-10-19).

### Win-back offers

**FACT:** Win-back offers are configured in App Store Connect for auto-renewable subscriptions and target previously subscribed, lapsed customers. Eligibility can depend on paid subscription duration, time since expiration, and the interval between redeemed offers. Family Sharing access alone does not qualify as having owned the subscription; an active subscription in the same group also makes the customer ineligible. [Apple win-back setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-win-back-offers), [supporting win-back offers](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/supporting-win-back-offers-in-your-app) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

**FACT:** Offers may appear in App Store placements, Apple Account subscription settings, through a direct URL, and in-app. In-app system messaging and StoreKit merchandising support iOS 18/iPadOS 18+; Apple lists macOS 15+ for the in-app sheet. The Manage Subscriptions placement has earlier OS availability. [Apple win-back setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-win-back-offers) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

### Promotional offers, offer codes, and introductory offers

- **Promotional offer — FACT:** A developer-created discount for eligible existing or former subscribers. Create it in App Store Connect, decide additional eligibility, and generate a server signature. Apple’s newer signature is a compact JWS; the signing key stays server-side. StoreKit’s JWS purchase option is back-deployed before the iOS 26/macOS 26 SDK generation. [JWS signing guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/generating-jws-to-sign-app-store-requests), [JWS purchase option](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/product/purchaseoption/promotionaloffer%28_%3Acompactjws%3A%29), [WWDC25 server API session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/249/) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).
- **Promotional offer at cancellation — FACT:** Apple Retention Messaging can attach a promotional offer to an approved real-time message. Its response includes an offer signature (V2 recommended by Apple). This is distinct from showing an offer in the app before opening Apple’s subscription manager. [Apple promotional-offer response](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/promotionaloffer) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).
- **RevenueCat Customer Center cancellation discount — FACT:** Configure Apple promotional offer IDs in App Store Connect and map them to Customer Center paths or survey answers in the RevenueCat dashboard. If no matching configured offer exists, the SDK proceeds with the requested action without showing that offer. [Customer Center configuration](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/customer-center/customer-center-configuration) (RevenueCat, checked 2026-10-19).
- **Offer codes — FACT:** App Store Connect creates codes for free or discounted In-App Purchases; people redeem them through a URL, the App Store, or supported in-app StoreKit redemption APIs. Apple documents APIs for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS; the API choice varies by platform. [Supporting offer codes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/supporting-offer-codes-in-your-app) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).
- **Introductory offers — FACT:** These are primarily acquisition offers for customers eligible under Apple’s subscription-group rules. They are configured in App Store Connect and must be described clearly in the purchase flow; don’t treat an introductory offer as a cancellation discount. [Auto-renewable subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/), [App Review Guidelines §3.1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (Apple, checked 2026-10-19).

## 4. Ranked project.json switches

**INFERENCE:** These are the 15 highest-value opt-in selections for the factory. “On” means generate the capability or contract only when the app’s chosen platforms and product justify it; it does not mean sending data or showing offers without the required user action and configuration.

1. `support.contact` — visible support URL/email and platform fallback.
2. `support.feedback` — in-app message with a preview of what will be sent.
3. `support.feedback_screenshot` — explicit, previewable screenshot attachment.
4. `commerce.customer_center` — RevenueCat self-service paths on supported UI platforms.
5. `commerce.cancellation_survey` — configurable reasons and survey response reporting.
6. `commerce.cancellation_offer` — per-reason or per-path promotional offer, off until configured.
7. `commerce.apple_retention_messaging` — Apple cancellation-flow messages/offers, off pending access and approval.
8. `commerce.win_back_offers` — eligible lapsed subscriber offer handling.
9. `commerce.billing_grace_period` — owner-configured grace handling and accurate recovery UI.
10. `commerce.billing_issue_messages` — StoreKit message listening/deferred presentation.
11. `account.deletion` — in-app account deletion when accounts are selected; must remain available.
12. `account.deletion_export` — optional export before deletion; never a prerequisite.
13. `support.feature_requests` — optional feature-request board/form link.
14. `lifecycle.whats_new` — versioned, dismissible change summary.
15. `engagement.review_prompt` — StoreKit request after a meaningful completion; never a custom prompt.
16. `support.quick_action` — Home Screen long-press quick action (`UIApplicationShortcutItem`, set at runtime): "Send feedback" for everyone; "Before you go" promotional offer for current or lapsed subscribers only. Mac: Dock menu item. Watch: none. **(Orchestrator addition; unverified.)**

**INFERENCE:** Keep data-safe expiry/read-only behavior, an empty-state first action, and undo within the relevant selected capability contracts; they protect the user’s work and should not be treated as retention switches. Keep referrals, alternate icons, trial-ending reminders, haptics, and broader analytics off unless the product has a concrete need.

**Skipped / limit:** I did not implement or test generated projects; this was read-only research. App deletion remains **UNKNOWN as an absolute claim**; treat it as unsupported by public Apple API and design around cancellation, billing, lapse, account deletion, and later win-back events.
