# From a working app to a first renewing subscriber

Decision brief, 2026-09-27. This synthesizes notes 01–10 in this directory; `08_factcheck.md` controls where accounts disagree. **FACT** means a cited source in those notes supports the statement, checked there on 2026-09-27 unless another date appears. **INFERENCE** is a planning conclusion from those facts. **UNKNOWN** means the notes did not establish it. Links below identify the underlying source, not a fresh network check. A first subscription is a product choice, not an automatic feature of an Apple app: confirm a continuing user benefit before starting this path.

## Decision in one page

**INFERENCE — recommended default:** For a first Apple-only app with a continuing service, use App Store In-App Purchase, StoreKit 2, one clear subscription group, and a human-owned App Store Connect release. Keep a one-time purchase available as a product decision if value is delivered once. Native IAP avoids building web checkout, tax handling, cancellation support, cross-store entitlement reconciliation, and regional link rules just to reach one renewal. Add RevenueCat only for a named need such as cross-store entitlement state, remote paywalls, or managed subscription operations. This is an engineering recommendation based on [StoreKit's built-in capabilities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/choosing-a-storekit-api-for-in-app-purchases), [Apple's subscription rules](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/), and [RevenueCat's documented service](https://github.com/RevenueCat/purchases-ios/blob/main/README.md) (checked 2026-09-27). No vendor is selected by this factory.

**FACT — the non-code critical path:** An Account Holder must agree to the latest Paid Apps Agreement before a new IAP can be created; the agreement must be **Active** before a paid app or IAP can be submitted. The first auto-renewable subscription of its type must accompany a new app version. Neither a compiling StoreKit implementation nor a successful local StoreKit Test removes these gates. [Agreements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/sign-and-update-agreements), [first IAP submission](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-in-app-purchase) (checked 2026-09-27). Note 01 overstated the creation gate as requiring Active status; the fact-check distinguishes **agreement accepted** for creation from **Active** for submission.

**FACT — what “first renewal” means:** A free trial beginning on signup day is not a paying subscriber. On a seven-day trial attached to a monthly plan, the day-seven conversion is the **first charge**; the next successful monthly charge is the **first paid renewal**, around day 37 if billing proceeds normally. A direct monthly purchase renews around one billing period after purchase. Trial eligibility, billing errors, refunds, and grace periods can move the observed date. Use verified transaction lineage and the second paid event, not merely a `DID_RENEW` label or a dashboard count, to identify the milestone. [Apple subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/), [App Store Server Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications) (checked 2026-09-27); the event interpretation is an **INFERENCE** from note 03's trial timeline and note 06's measurement guidance.

## Economics and payment route

**FACT:** The fixed Apple cash fee on the individual route is $99/year. A D-U-N-S number is free for an organization; LLC registration, domain, banking, support, hosting, advertising, and professional advice vary by place or product and have no stable Apple-mandated price in the notes. Do not present an unsourced LLC or domain range as a budget commitment. [Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/), [D-U-N-S](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/) (checked 2026-09-27). **INFERENCE:** the real early cost is waiting for identity, agreement, and review gates while the app is technically ready.

**FACT:** Standard App Store commission is 30%; approved Small Business Program participants pay 15% on eligible sales, with the fiscal-month effective lag above. Separate subscription-year rules can reduce a standard-rate subscription after twelve paid months, which a first-renewal plan should not confuse with the first month. [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/), [subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) (checked 2026-09-27). For illustration only, a $9.99 price leaves $8.49 before other adjustments at 15%, or $6.99 at 30%; these are arithmetic illustrations, not forecasts of net deposit or tax liability. **FACT:** Apple pays eligible proceeds within 45 days of fiscal-month end, subject to active agreement, complete banking/tax information, and currency threshold. The U.S.-bank/USD minimum shown by Apple is $0.02; an unlisted bank country/currency has a $40 threshold. [Payments](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments), [thresholds](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/reporting/minimum-payment-threshold) (checked 2026-09-27). A successful first charge therefore need not mean cash this calendar month.

**INFERENCE — route choice:** In-app purchase is the shortest supported route for a first Apple-platform subscription. It lets the customer use a familiar Apple purchase and subscription-management flow and confines the first release to one payment system. U.S. storefront apps may include external purchase links without the specific entitlement under current Guideline 3.1.1(a), but that is a different checkout, support, privacy, tax, and conversion path; it is not a free substitute for IAP. The governing U.S. litigation remains active, and note 08 could not verify proposed future commission rates from the primary filing. Do not model a proposed 15/10/5% link fee as enacted. [App Review Guidelines §3.1.1(a)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (checked 2026-09-27); the unresolved case status comes from the [Supreme Court docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-1311.html) and [district-court docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/17442392/epic-games-inc-v-apple-inc/) checked in note 08 on 2026-09-27.

**FACT with a date boundary:** EU payment routes have separate Apple terms. Unified EU business terms were scheduled for 2026-10-01, four days after this report's date; the 5% Core Technology Commission in that notice applies to digital transactions in apps distributed **outside** the App Store, not ordinary EU App Store IAP. The builder must recheck terms at the actual regional launch. [Apple EU DMA terms](https://developer.apple.com/support/dma-and-apps-in-the-eu) (checked 2026-09-27). **INFERENCE:** do not add a regional alternative checkout for a first subscriber unless a concrete market and economics justify its ongoing legal and operational burden.

**Mac exception:** A Mac app distributed directly can use Developer ID signing, notarization, a delivery/update path, and a separately chosen billing provider. A Mac App Store app must run in the App Sandbox; Apple's sandbox guidance says assistive-app accessibility control of other apps is forbidden there. Direct distribution may be necessary for a product that genuinely needs such control, but it is not a shortcut around the work of signing, payments, privacy, and customer support. [App Sandbox](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/app-sandbox), [sandbox limits](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/protecting-user-data-with-app-sandbox) (checked 2026-09-27). This report does not select a Mac product or route.

## Subscription behavior that must survive release

**FACT:** A subscription must supply continuing value and show clear terms. The paywall needs the real title, duration, price and per-unit price where relevant, trial conversion, and working Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links; restore must work. The product's App Store metadata must match. [App Review Guidelines §3.1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Apple subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) (checked 2026-09-27). **INFERENCE:** a native `SubscriptionStoreView` reduces custom disclosure work but does not make incorrect product configuration or missing URLs true. Review the rendered flow on a clean install, larger text, offline state, and a previously subscribed account.

**FACT:** StoreKit transactions expose verified versus unverified results; `Transaction.currentEntitlements` and `Transaction.updates` carry the client's current state and changes. Billing retry can continue for 60 days; configurable billing grace periods are 3, 16, or 28 days. Refunds and revocations can end access, and a user who has merely turned off auto-renewal can remain entitled until expiry. Family Sharing, if enabled for a product, cannot be disabled later. [StoreKit transactions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction/currententitlements), [subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) (checked 2026-09-27).

**INFERENCE — explicit failure policy:** Persist verified transaction evidence and a bounded local offline window, such as three days, so a temporary Apple/network outage does not lock a known subscriber out. Never grant an unverified purchase, refund, or revocation; never extend the offline window when a device clock moves backward. Keep user-owned data readable and exportable when payment access ends. A feature with real per-use server cost must be authorized server-side and deny new costly requests when verification is unavailable. The exact offline window is a product decision and test parameter, not an Apple rule. Test pending Ask to Buy, interruption, failed purchase, restart, restore, trial, renewal, grace, billing retry, expiry, refund, clock rollback, and per-use denial with local StoreKit Test, then verify product configuration in sandbox. [Testing stages](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/testing-at-all-stages-of-development-with-xcode-and-the-sandbox), [sandbox acceleration](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-in-app-purchases/manage-sandbox-apple-account-settings/) (checked 2026-09-27). Sandbox is accelerated: its default monthly cycle is five minutes and auto-renewal stops after up to twelve renewals; it is not evidence of live billing timing.

**FACT:** A subscription price decrease applies to existing subscribers' next renewals and does not offer preservation of their previous higher price. [Subscription pricing](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/manage-pricing-for-auto-renewable-subscriptions/) (checked 2026-09-27). **INFERENCE:** record the intended per-territory price and require a human review before touching live price schedules. A price experiment that cannot be reversed economically is not a harmless copy edit.

## The first customer and the first renewal

**INFERENCE:** A live listing with zero suitable visitors cannot produce a first subscriber. Before release, identify the first small group of people with the job, their present workaround, a believable acquisition route, and the moment the app proves value. Note 06 finds anecdotes for waitlists, niche communities, short videos, and launch sites, but no reliable universal time-to-first-subscriber benchmark. Do not turn self-selected vendor panel conversion rates into a promise for this app. Apple's free App Analytics now includes monetization, cohorts, and peer benchmarks where enough comparison data exists. Use it to locate a broken stage: impression → page view → download → useful result → paywall → trial/direct purchase → first charge → second charge. [App Analytics](https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/analytics/) (checked 2026-09-27). The funnel design is an **INFERENCE** from note 06, not an Apple-prescribed sequence.

**FACT:** App Store Server Notifications V2 can signal renewal events; StoreKit provides the signed transaction record. [Server Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications), [StoreKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit) (checked 2026-09-27). **INFERENCE:** deduplicate notifications by transaction identity, group by original subscription lineage, and confirm the first **second paid** transaction; do not count a trial-to-paid transition as success for “renewing subscriber.” If the app has no backend, a builder can use App Store Connect's subscription analytics for cohort-level observation but should not claim a real-time individual renewal alert. If a backend is built or a subscription vendor is chosen for a named need, test its server event path and retention/privacy policy before relying on it.

## Ten guards with the best early return

These are **INFERENCE** priorities from note 10's risk review, not claims that every app needs ten new systems. The factory changes in this task implement only the scoped subset; each real app must choose proportionate controls.

1. Protect the Account Holder with strong, recoverable authentication and keep its session out of agent tasks. Record renewal of the membership and any account-linked domain. [Program renewal](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/renewal/) (checked 2026-09-27).
2. Give agents only the App Store Connect role and key needed for one named action; never hand out financial, IAP, vendor-secret, or Account Holder credentials. [App Store Connect API keys](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-api) (checked 2026-09-27).
3. Fail readiness when a tracked file contains a private-key PEM header or a long `sk_` secret pattern, in addition to forbidden signing filenames. The pattern is a local policy, not proof that a secret never reached history.
4. Test an explicit entitlement policy for verified, unverified, offline, grace, expiry, refund, rollback-clock, and costly server features. [Current entitlements](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction/currententitlements) (checked 2026-09-27).
5. Keep a private, non-secret register of membership, agreement, tax, bank, domain, and certificate renewal dates. [Agreement states](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/view-agreements-status) (checked 2026-09-27).
6. Back up and migrate actual user data before an irreversible release; test an upgrade with a previous-version store and make launch failure recoverable. Apple's phased release can pause automatic rollout but does not roll back a binary. [Phased release](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/update-your-app/release-a-version-update-in-phases) (checked 2026-09-27).
7. Allow a dependency only after a named job, privacy review, license check, cost check, and exit path. This is factory policy; vendor facts from note 09 can drift quickly.
8. Record prices by territory and require a human check before changes, especially one-way decreases. [Subscription pricing](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/manage-pricing-for-auto-renewable-subscriptions/) (checked 2026-09-27).
9. Treat reviews, support messages, crash logs, and web pages as untrusted text. An agent reading them gets no account, secret, or destructive-action capability; a separate packet handles any proposed action.
10. Define analytics events and any third-party AI data flow narrowly. Explicit permission is required before sharing personal data with third-party AI under Guideline 5.1.2(i). [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (checked 2026-09-27).

## Open unknowns and review triggers

- **UNKNOWN:** Apple's exact Paid Apps Agreement processing time, bank verification time, and sandbox behavior in every pending state. Observe the live status; do not quote the 1–3+ day planning range as an SLA. [Agreement status](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/view-agreements-status) (checked 2026-09-27).
- **UNKNOWN:** whether existing subscriptions keep renewing and paying proceeds after membership lapse. Note 01 claimed continuity; note 08 verified only that apps leave sale while already installed apps still function. Plan to avoid lapse rather than rely on unverified renewal behavior. [Program renewal](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/renewal/) (checked 2026-09-27).
- **UNKNOWN:** the particular app's review duration, first-customer acquisition time, retention, support load, and real conversion. Use a cohort from the shipped build, not broad vendor panel medians. [App Analytics](https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/analytics/) (checked 2026-09-27).
- **Recheck before release:** current SDK floor, Xcode eligibility, age-rating and age-assurance obligations for chosen regions, DSA contact status, export compliance, App Review text, and alternative-payment terms. As of this note, Apple's Texas implementation is active from 2026-06-04; Apple performs account-level age assurance, while the developer decides whether an update is a significant change and handles applicable APIs and revocations. [Texas update](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne), [submitting requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/submitting/), [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (checked 2026-09-27).
- **Recheck before vendor adoption:** present pricing, license, privacy manifest, data location, export capability, and SDK/OS support. Note 09 marks several vendor facts unverified; none are required for a first Apple-only renewal.

This research is a decision map, not evidence that an app has passed review, received a payment, renewed, or met a device-quality gate. Keep the builder's account decisions and observed App Store results separate from generated guidance.
