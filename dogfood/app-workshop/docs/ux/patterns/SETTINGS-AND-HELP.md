# Settings and help

Provide defaults that let most people do the job immediately. Put a task-specific option beside the task it changes. Use a settings area for infrequent app-wide choices. Follow the selected platform's normal route; a Mac app with custom settings uses the App menu and a Settings scene or window.

## Inventory

| Choice or help need | Default | Where it lives | Save and recovery behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending |

Use the system's appearance, language, accessibility, and privacy choices unless a distinct product need calls for another option. Give settings clear names and show their effect. For destructive reset or data removal, name the scope and provide confirmation or undo appropriate to the loss. Keep export, account deletion, purchase restoration, support, and privacy links findable when those features exist.

Help should answer the current problem: a short explanation near an unfamiliar control, an error with recovery, and a durable support route. Test Settings with keyboard and assistive input, relaunch to confirm persistence, and verify that each setting changes the documented behavior on every selected platform.

Apple sources checked 2026-09-25: [Settings HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/settings), [Offering help](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/offering-help), [SwiftUI Settings scene](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/settings).
