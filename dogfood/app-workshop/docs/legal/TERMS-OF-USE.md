> Draft — not legal advice; have it reviewed

**You may use Apple's standard EULA instead of writing your own.** Apple applies its standard license agreement in every region unless you supply a custom one in App Store Connect (App Information, License Agreement). If you use the standard EULA, the Terms of Use link can point to it and this page can shrink to your subscription terms. Write custom terms only when you need them, and have them reviewed.

## Subscription apps: links are required
An app that sells auto-renewable subscriptions needs functional Terms of Use and privacy policy links in the purchase flow inside the app and in the App Store metadata. A missing metadata link is a common avoidable rejection. [Guideline 3.1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) points to Schedule 2 of the Apple Developer Program License Agreement for the exact requirements; confirm current wording there before submitting (checked 2026-09-27). Skip this section if the app has no subscriptions.
